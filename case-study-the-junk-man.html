The Junk Man case study

An online-booking-first homepage for a Calgary junk removal company.

View the live design

  • ClientThe Junk Man
  • LocationCalgary, AB
  • IndustryJunk removal
  • What we didHomepage design
The goal

What the homepage had to do.

Junk removal customers are often in a hurry and unsure what it will cost. The homepage needed to promise a fast online booking, explain the pricing simply and show real results.

What we designed

Inside the homepage design.

Book online in minutes

The hero headline and buttons lead straight to online booking, a free photo quote, or a call or text.

Recognition and rating strip

Awards, ratings and a family-owned note sit near the top to build confidence early.

Simple pricing section

Pricing is explained in plain words, including that loading and cleanup are included, with a short explainer on how it works.

Three steps, zero hassle

Upload photos, add location and job details, choose a load size and pick an appointment time.

Before and after gallery

Real job photos show the difference, with a link to see more.

Other services and a closing call to action

Commercial cleanouts, hauling and seasonal services are listed, and the page ends with service area and donation details.

Results

How a new website, local SEO and Google Ads grow inquiries.

What drove the growth

New website

A quote-first design with a clear call to action, fast mobile pages and visible trust signals turns more visitors into quote requests.

Local SEO

Service pages, a complete Google Business Profile and local content help the company appear when nearby customers search.

Google Ads

Search campaigns aimed at high-intent searches bring in leads while organic rankings build up.
