Mr. Steam Calgary case study

A bright, price-first homepage for a Calgary carpet and upholstery cleaner.

View the live design

  • ClientMr. Steam Calgary
  • LocationCalgary, AB
  • IndustryCarpet and upholstery cleaning
  • What we didHomepage design
The goal

What the homepage had to do.

Carpet and upholstery customers want to know the price and how to book before they commit. The homepage needed to show starting prices, build trust with a friendly brand and make booking or a text quick.

What we designed

Inside the homepage design.

Clear hero with two actions

The headline names the services, a checklist lists what is covered, and Book now and Call or text buttons sit side by side.

Meet the team section

A real team and van photo with short proof points (local, homes and businesses, free quotes) puts faces behind the brand.

Six reasons to choose them

Eco-friendly products, pet and kid safe, professional equipment, posted prices, homes and businesses, and easy booking.

Service cards with starting prices

Carpet, upholstery, area rugs, mattresses and commercial carpet are each shown with a starting price or a quote note.

Price estimator and booking path

A see-your-price-first section leads to an online booking page, with a simple three-step explanation of how it works.

Photo-friendly quote form

The quote form accepts up to six photos and opens the customer's messaging app with the details ready to send.

What drives inquiries

How a new website, local SEO and Google Ads grow inquiries.

Three services work together to bring more of the right visitors to the site and turn more of them into quote requests.

New website

A quote-first design with a clear call to action, fast mobile pages and visible trust signals turns more visitors into quote requests.

Local SEO

Service pages, a complete Google Business Profile and local content help the company appear when nearby customers search.

Google Ads

Search campaigns aimed at high-intent searches bring in leads while organic rankings build up.
