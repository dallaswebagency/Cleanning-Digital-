How we work

Remote, focused and led by Nakul.

CleaningDigital works only with cleaning companies, and we work with Calgary-area owners remotely by video call and email. We do not claim a Calgary office or local team. What we bring is ten years of web design and digital marketing experience from our founder, Nakul Chandra, on top of experience from a Dallas-based agency.

If you run a residential, commercial, maid or janitorial company in the Calgary area, the goal is simple: make it easier for the right customers to find you, trust you and ask for a quote.

What Calgary cleaning companies need online

Clear service areas, honest pages and an easy quote path.

Service areas that match reality

Calgary customers search by quadrant and neighbourhood. List the places you actually serve, such as NW Calgary, Beltline, Marda Loop, Airdrie or Cochrane if they apply, and build a distinct page only where you can say something useful.

Pages for each type of work

Home cleaning, move-out cleaning, office cleaning and janitorial work attract different customers. Separate pages let each one ask the right questions and show the right proof.

A quote path that works on a phone

Most people compare cleaners on their phones. A short form, a tap-to-call button and clear next steps reduce the chance they leave for a competitor.

Do you have an office in Calgary?

No. CleaningDigital works remotely with cleaning companies by video call and email. Our approach builds on the experience of a Dallas-based agency, and Nakul Chandra leads each project. We have already designed websites for many Calgary cleaning companies.

Can you guarantee Calgary rankings?

No. Search results depend on competition, your website, your profile and many other factors, and no one can honestly guarantee a position. We focus on the work that helps you appear for the right searches.

Will you build a page for every Calgary neighbourhood?

Only for areas you genuinely serve and where we can write something useful. Copying one page and swapping the city name rarely helps customers or search engines.

Residential, commercial, maid service, janitorial and specialty cleaners such as carpet, window and post-construction.
