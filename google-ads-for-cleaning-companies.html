Service guide

What to expect from google ads.

The practical details cleaning company owners usually ask before they decide.

Who it suits

  • Cleaning companies that want inquiries faster than SEO alone can deliver.
  • Owners who can take on more work and want better-qualified calls.
  • Businesses with a clear service area and a website ready to convert visitors.

What is included

  • Campaign structure built around the services you want most.
  • Keyword and location targeting, with negative keywords to filter out irrelevant searches.
  • Ad copy written for cleaning customers.
  • Call and form tracking so leads can be counted.
  • Regular reviews of search terms, spend and lead quality.

What is not included

  • Your ad budget, which is paid directly to Google.
  • Guaranteed lead volume or cost per lead.
  • Fixing a website that cannot convert visitors, which is the web design service.

What we need from you

  • A monthly ad budget you are comfortable with.
  • Your best-value services and the areas you serve.
  • Access to your Google Ads account or help creating one.
  • Information on which jobs you do not want.

How success is measured

  • Calls and form leads from ads, once tracking is set up.
  • Cost per lead and lead quality over time.
  • Search terms that bring in the right inquiries and those to exclude.

What affects timelines and results

  • Competition in your area and your budget.
  • How well the landing page turns clicks into quote requests.
  • Quality of tracking, which determines what can be measured.

Practical examples

  • Ads for "cleaning jobs" bring in job seekers instead of customers. Negative keywords remove them.
  • Ads sending people to a slow, cluttered page waste budget. A focused landing page with a short form and call button helps.

How it works with other services

  • Web design. Ads send traffic to your pages, so design affects how many clicks become leads.
  • Local SEO. Ads can bring inquiries while organic rankings take time to build.
  • Meta ads. Meta ads reach people who are not searching yet, while Google Ads reaches people who are.

How much should a cleaning company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your services and competition. We recommend a budget that gathers enough data to learn, then scale what works. We confirm figures after a discovery call.

What is PPC management for cleaning companies?

PPC, or pay-per-click, management means building, monitoring and improving paid search campaigns so more of your spend turns into calls and quote requests.

Can you guarantee how many leads I will get?

No honest agency can. We share expected ranges from the data, track every lead and optimize based on results.

Do you charge a percentage of ad spend?

We confirm our management fee clearly in your proposal, with no hidden charges. Your ad budget goes directly to Google.

Do you run ads in other cities?
