What does website maintenance include?

Typically updates, backups, security monitoring, speed and uptime checks, form tests and a set number of small edits each month.

Can you maintain a website you did not build?

Yes. We review it first, fix urgent issues and then place it on a care plan.

How quickly do you respond to problems?

Urgent issues such as a down site or broken form receive priority attention. We confirm response times in your plan.

Do I need a maintenance plan if my site is new?

A new site still needs updates, backups and monitoring. A plan keeps it safe and helps it keep performing.

Do you support websites outside Calgary?

Yes. We support cleaning company websites in Edmonton, Vancouver, Toronto and across Canada.
