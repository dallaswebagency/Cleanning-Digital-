FAQ

Questions cleaning company owners ask.

Do you only work with cleaning companies?

Yes. Every website and marketing plan we build is for cleaning businesses, so we already understand how your customers search, what they worry about and what makes them request a quote.

How long does a new cleaning website take?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly we receive your logo, photos and service details. We agree a timeline with you on the discovery call, before any work starts.

Can you guarantee my website will rank on Google?

No one can honestly guarantee rankings. We build every site with strong local SEO foundations, including clean structure, fast loading, service and city pages and schema markup, then improve it over time. Results depend on your market and competition.

Can you help if I already have a website?

Yes. We can review your current site and tell you whether targeted fixes or a redesign makes more sense. You can ask for a free website review when you book a discovery call.

Do you work with businesses in both the USA and Canada?

Yes. We work with cleaning companies across both countries and tailor spelling, service pages and local SEO to your market.

What happens after the website launches?

We can continue with ongoing support and maintenance, local SEO, AI SEO and regular improvements, or hand the site over so you can manage it yourself. We confirm scope and cost with you in advance.
