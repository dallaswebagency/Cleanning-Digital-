Local SEO for Cleaning Companies: A Simple 90 Day Plan

By Nakul Chandra, Founder and Lead Strategist · October 25, 2026 · 6 min read

Local SEO is how cleaning companies show up when someone searches for a service near them. You do not need a huge budget to start. You need a clear plan and steady follow-through. Our local SEO and Google Business Profile services follow this same plan.

Days 1 to 30: Get the foundation right

Claim and fully complete your Google Business Profile. Choose accurate categories, add every service, set your service areas and upload real photos. Make sure your business name, address and phone number match across your website and directories.

Days 31 to 60: Build service and city pages

Create a page for each core service and for each city you want to serve. Write original content that explains what you do, who it is for and what to expect. Add a quote form and your contact details to every page.

Days 61 to 90: Earn reviews and refine

Ask every happy customer for a Google review and make it easy with a direct link. Reply to reviews. Review your search performance, improve pages that get impressions but few clicks, and add helpful blog posts that answer common questions.

What to track

Watch calls, form submissions, Google Business Profile actions and the search terms that bring visitors. Rankings matter, but leads are the real measure.

Before day one: set your goal and baseline

Spend a day on measurement before you change anything. Record how many calls, forms and direction requests you get in a normal month. Take screenshots of where you appear for five searches, such as “house cleaning Calgary” or “office cleaning Aurora IL.” Install Search Console and link your Google Business Profile insights. Write down your best three services by profit, not just by volume, because local SEO should push the jobs you actually want. Choose a realistic target, like more quote requests from two neighbourhoods. Without a baseline you will not know whether the next ninety days worked. Choose keywords by service and place

Local SEO for cleaning companies works when each page targets one service in one area. Build a simple list. Column one: services such as deep cleaning, move out cleaning, office cleaning and recurring home cleaning. Column two: places such as NW Calgary, Airdrie, Cochrane, Okotoks, North Aurora, Naperville or the Fox Valley. Combine them and keep the combinations that match where you really send crews. Skip areas you cannot serve on time. Then check what shows up on Google for each phrase. If the results are all big directories, you may need a stronger page or a different angle. Our local SEO service includes this research, and the Calgary client acquisition guide shows how the pieces fit together.

Tidy your citations and listings in week two

Citations are mentions of your business name, address and phone number on other sites. Search for your phone number and your name on Google and write down every listing you find. Fix anything with an old address, a former name or a tracking number that differs from your main one. Then claim the important ones: Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Facebook and the main directories for your country. In Canada that includes local business directories and chamber of commerce pages. In Illinois, check the chamber of commerce for North Aurora or Aurora and local business groups. Use one exact format for your name and address everywhere. Do not buy huge bundles of low quality listings, because quality and consistency matter more than volume.

Write pages that answer real questions

A service page should read like a helpful conversation. Start with who the service is for. Explain what is included and what is not. Describe how a visit works from booking to the final walk through. Mention how you handle keys, alarms, pets and special requests. Add a short FAQ and a clear quote button. Aim for useful depth rather than a word count target. Add your neighbourhoods naturally, such as “we clean homes in Tuscany, Royal Oak and Evanston in NW Calgary,” instead of listing fifty places in a block. Include a few real photos with descriptive alt text. Link between related pages so visitors and search engines see how they connect. Use Google Business Profile as a weekly habit

Your profile is not a set and forget task. Each week, add one photo of a real job or team moment, answer new questions and check that hours and holiday hours are correct. Each month, post an update about a seasonal offer, such as spring cleaning in Calgary after a long winter or pre holiday cleaning in the Fox Valley. Review the services list and the categories. Look at the profile insights to see which searches bring views, calls and direction requests. If your service area is large, resist the urge to add every town. Keep it to the places you serve well. Our Google Business Profile tips for Calgary go deeper on categories, services and photos, and the profile optimization service covers the setup.

Build local links the honest way

Links from trusted local sites tell Google your business is real and active. You do not need hundreds. Start with places that already know you: your chamber of commerce, a supplier or franchise partner page, local business associations, community sponsorships and charity events you help with. Offer a useful tip for a local blog or newsletter, such as how property managers can prepare for move out inspections. Ask clients with business sites, like dental offices or gyms, whether they would mention you as their cleaning provider. Never buy link packages or join link swaps that look spammy, as they can cause harm. A handful of relevant local links earned over months is better than a quick burst that looks unnatural.

Make reviews a repeatable system

Reviews work best when you ask at the right moment and in the same way every time. Send the request within a few hours of finishing a job, while the home still looks great. Use a short message and a direct link to your Google review form. Train crew leads to mention it at the end of a visit. Reply to every review, using the customer's first name and one detail from the job. Handle negative reviews calmly, thank them, apologize where fair and offer to fix the problem offline. Never offer rewards for reviews, because it breaks Google's rules. Our post on getting more Google reviews includes wording you can adapt, and the reviews page shows what good proof looks like.

Review and adjust at day 90

At the end of the quarter, compare your numbers with the baseline. Look at calls, forms, direction requests and the search terms that bring impressions. Find pages with many impressions and few clicks, then improve their titles and opening paragraphs. Find pages with clicks and no quotes, then improve the offer and the form. Decide what to repeat: more service pages, more neighbourhood pages, more reviews or more local links. Be honest about what did not work, because every market is different and rankings depend on competition. Then plan the next ninety days. Fix the technical basics early

Technical problems can quietly hold back good content. Check that your site uses HTTPS, loads fast on a phone and has no broken pages or duplicate versions of the home page. Submit an XML sitemap in Search Console and look for crawl errors. Make sure every page has a unique title and description that mention the service and the place. Add local business schema with your name, phone, service area and hours so search engines read your details correctly. Keep your phone number as real text, not an image, so people can tap it. Plan for AI search at the same time

More people ask ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews who to hire. The same habits that help local SEO also help here: consistent details, clear service pages, honest FAQs and genuine reviews. Write short, direct answers to common questions such as what a deep clean includes or how often to book recurring service. Keep your business facts the same across your site and listings. You do not need a separate strategy in the first ninety days, just clean content and trust signals. Frequently Asked Questions

How long does local SEO take to work?

Timelines vary by market and competition, and no one can honestly guarantee rankings. Local SEO is an ongoing effort rather than a one-time task, and steady progress usually builds over several months.

Do I need a separate page for every city?

If you serve several cities, a unique page for each important one helps. Avoid copying the same text with the city name swapped, because that adds little value.

Are Google reviews important for rankings?

Reviews help with both trust and local visibility. A steady flow of genuine reviews and thoughtful replies is better than a sudden burst.

Can I do local SEO myself?

You can handle the basics, such as your profile and review requests. Many owners bring in help for page strategy, technical fixes and consistent monthly work.

Can small cleaning companies compete with large franchises in local SEO?

Yes, because local search rewards relevance, proximity and reviews. A small company with a complete profile, clear service pages and recent reviews can often win in specific neighbourhoods, even if a big brand dominates the broader city search.

How many service pages should I build in the first 90 days?

Start with your three most profitable services and your two most important areas. Build them well, with useful detail and a quote button, then expand. Quality pages perform better than a pile of thin ones.

Do I need a blog for local SEO?

A blog is not required, but helpful posts can answer questions and support your service pages. Write about real customer questions, such as how often to book recurring cleaning, and link back to the matching service page.

What should I do if my rankings drop?

Check for changes first: a profile suspension, a website error, lost listings or a competitor surge. Review your recent edits and Search Console. Avoid panic changes. Small, steady fixes and fresh reviews usually help more than overhauling everything at once.
