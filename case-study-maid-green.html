Maid Green case study

A quote-first homepage for an eco-friendly cleaning company serving the Fox Valley.

View the live design

  • ClientMaid Green
  • LocationNorth Aurora, IL
  • IndustryResidential and commercial cleaning
  • What we didHomepage design
The goal

What the homepage had to do.

Maid Green serves homes and businesses across the Fox Valley and leans on a trusted, eco-friendly brand. The homepage needed to make that trust obvious in seconds and put a free quote one tap away.

“We relied on referrals and struggled to get leads online. Nakul helped turn our website into a source of cleaning inquiries.”Petter, Owner of Maid Green Cleaning
What we designed

Inside the homepage design.

Quote form in the hero

A short form (name, phone, ZIP, service) sits beside the headline, with a click-to-call number next to it, so a visitor can act without scrolling.

Trust bar under the hero

Google rating, review count, satisfaction guarantee, vetted crews and non-toxic products sit in one row directly below the fold.

Home and business paths

A split section sends residential and commercial visitors to the right services and the right quote button.

Eight service cards

House cleaning, office cleaning, window cleaning, move in and move out, carpet, deep cleaning, same-day and post-construction, each with its own link.

Local service area section

Twelve Fox Valley communities are listed so nearby customers can confirm coverage quickly.

Reviews, FAQ and a second quote form

Google review cards and a FAQ answer common worries, and a full quote form with bedrooms and bathrooms closes the page.

What drives inquiries

How a new website, local SEO and Google Ads grow inquiries.

Three services work together to bring more of the right visitors to the site and turn more of them into quote requests.

New website

A quote-first design with a clear call to action, fast mobile pages and visible trust signals turns more visitors into quote requests.

Local SEO

Service pages, a complete Google Business Profile and local content help the company appear when nearby customers search.

Google Ads

Search campaigns aimed at high-intent searches bring in leads while organic rankings build up.
