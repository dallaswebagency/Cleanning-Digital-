How we work

Remote, focused and led by Nakul.

CleaningDigital works only with cleaning companies, and we work with Dallas-area owners remotely by video call and email. We do not claim a Dallas office or local team. What we bring is ten years of web design and digital marketing experience from our founder, Nakul Chandra, on top of experience from a Dallas-based agency.

If you run a residential, commercial, maid or janitorial company in the Dallas area, the goal is simple: make it easier for the right customers to find you, trust you and ask for a quote.

What Dallas cleaning companies need online

Clear service areas, honest pages and an easy quote path.

Service areas that match reality

The Dallas area spans many cities and neighbourhoods. List the places you actually serve, such as Dallas, Plano, Irving, Frisco, Arlington or Fort Worth if they apply, and build a distinct page only where you can say something useful.

Pages for each type of work

Home cleaning, move-out cleaning, office cleaning and janitorial work attract different customers. Separate pages let each one ask the right questions and show the right proof.

A quote path that works on a phone

Most people compare cleaners on their phones. A short form, a tap-to-call button and clear next steps reduce the chance they leave for a competitor.

Our approach

A simple plan, in the right order.

  1. Review. We look at your website, Google Business Profile and the way customers reach you today.
  2. Fix the foundation. A clear website with service pages and a short quote path comes first.
  3. Improve visibility. Local SEO and a complete Google profile help nearby customers find you.
