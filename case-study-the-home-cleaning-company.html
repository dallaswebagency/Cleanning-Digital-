The Home Cleaning Company case study

A trust-first homepage for a long-established Calgary home cleaning company.

View the live design

  • ClientThe Home Cleaning Company
  • LocationCalgary, AB
  • IndustryResidential and commercial cleaning
  • What we didHomepage design
The goal

What the homepage had to do.

This company has served Calgary families for many years and its strongest asset is trust. The homepage needed to show that history, the credentials behind it and a simple way to book.

What we designed

Inside the homepage design.

Trust-led hero

Calgary's home cleaners since 2001, with a call button, an online booking button and a row for experienced, background checked, insured and bonded.

Credentials made visible

Insurance, workers' compensation cover, a fidelity bond and key security are shown as clear tiles rather than buried in small print.

Meet the owner

A short section introduces the owner and manager, so customers know who is behind the team.

Home and business services

Residential, commercial, move in and move out, carpet cleaning and helper services each get their own card.

Flat-rate pricing cards

Simple pricing cards for common home sizes, with the most popular option highlighted and the terms stated plainly.

Three-step booking, reviews and FAQ

Book, clean, relax, followed by customer reviews and a FAQ that covers supplies, insurance and pets.
