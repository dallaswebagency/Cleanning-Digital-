What is an AI receptionist for a cleaning company?

It is an AI assistant that answers calls, texts, web chats and messages for your cleaning company, around the clock. It answers common questions, collects the details of the job, books appointments with a connected calendar and passes the conversation to your team when needed.

Which channels can it cover?

Web chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram messages, email and phone calls. Which of these you get depends on the plan. Chat, WhatsApp, social messages and email are in every plan. Texting with a local number and missed call text-back start on the Professional plan. Answering phone calls with AI voice is on the Premium plan.

What happens when someone calls and nobody picks up?

On Professional and Premium plans, missed call text-back sends the caller a text straight away so the lead is not lost. On Premium, the AI can answer the call itself, answer questions about your services and areas, and collect the job details.

Will it know my services, prices and service area?

Yes. We set it up with your real hours, services, service areas, FAQs and policies before it goes live. If it does not know an answer, it can hand the conversation to your team instead of guessing.

Can it book appointments?

Yes. It can book appointments with a connected calendar and capture lead details through chat, and on the Premium plan through phone calls too.

Can it reply in other languages?

Yes. It can reply in multiple languages, which helps when customers or cleaning staff prefer to write in another language.

Does it replace my team?

No. It handles the first reply and the routine questions so your team can focus on the cleaning. Your team can step into any conversation from the shared inbox, and every chat is saved against the contact.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the plan and the channels you need, plus setup. We scope it on a free discovery call so you only pay for what your company will use.
