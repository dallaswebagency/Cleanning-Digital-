What is AI SEO?

AI SEO, also called generative engine optimization, is making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand and recommend.

Can you guarantee my cleaning company will appear in ChatGPT?

No one can guarantee that. We improve the signals AI tools rely on, such as clear content, structured data, reviews and consistent facts, and we measure the changes.

Is AI SEO different from local SEO?

They overlap. Local SEO helps you rank in Google and Maps, and AI SEO adds answer-ready content and structured facts so AI tools can quote you accurately.

Do I need new content for AI SEO?

Often a small set of strong pages and FAQs is enough. We start with your existing site and add only what is missing.

Do you offer AI SEO in other cities?

Yes. We work with cleaning companies in other regions too.
