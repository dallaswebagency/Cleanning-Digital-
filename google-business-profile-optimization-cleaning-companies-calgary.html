How do I rank higher on Google Maps in Calgary?

Maps rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence. We improve relevance with correct categories and content, and prominence with reviews, citations and a strong website.

How many reviews does a cleaning company need?

There is no fixed number. Steady, recent, genuine reviews matter more than a one-time burst, so we build a simple routine.

Can I have a profile if I work from home and travel to customers?

Yes. Service area businesses can hide their address and list the areas they serve, as long as they follow Google's rules.

Can you fix a suspended profile?

We can often help with reinstatement requests and compliance fixes, though Google makes the final decision.

Do you optimize profiles outside Calgary?

Yes. We also work with cleaning companies in Edmonton, Vancouver, Toronto and other Canadian cities.
