Google Ads vs Meta Ads for Cleaning Companies in Calgary: Which Should You Start With?

By the CleaningDigital Team · October 25, 2026 · 6 min read

Both channels can fill your calendar, but they work differently. Understanding the difference helps you spend the first dollar well.

Google Ads reach people who are searching

Someone typing house cleaning Calgary or commercial cleaners near me is ready to hire. That intent is why Google Ads for cleaning companies often delivers the fastest leads.

Meta ads reach people before they search

Facebook and Instagram let you show an offer, such as spring cleaning or move-out cleaning, to homeowners in your area. See our Meta ads service for how we build them.

Cost and competition

Costs depend on service, season and competition, and they change often, so we do not quote fixed numbers. We start with a test budget, learn and scale.

Creative and offers

Google needs strong keywords and ad copy. Meta needs strong images and a clear offer. Real photos of your team and results usually outperform stock.

Tracking and follow-up

Track calls and form fills for both. Fast follow-up matters even more with Meta leads, which are often less ready to buy. A booking system helps convert them.

Which should you start with?

If you need leads quickly and have a good site, start with Google Ads. If you want to build awareness or fill recurring slots, add Meta. Many Calgary cleaners use both alongside local SEO. Not sure? Book a discovery call and we will recommend a plan.

Set up your first Google Ads campaign step by step

Start small and keep it tight. Step one: pick one service, such as house cleaning or office cleaning, and build one campaign around it. Step two: choose a handful of keywords that show clear buying intent, like cleaning service Calgary or move out cleaning near me. Step three: set your location to the areas you really serve, for example NW and NE Calgary first, then add Airdrie or Cochrane later. Step four: write ads that match what people typed and point them to a page about that exact service. Step five: add negative keywords so you do not pay for jobs, free, DIY or training searches. Step six: set a daily budget you are comfortable testing for a few weeks. Our Google Ads management for cleaning companies follows this same simple path, and a page built for the click matters just as much, which we cover in why cleaning websites do not get quote requests.

Set up your first Meta campaign step by step

Meta works best when you give it one clear offer and one clear audience. Pick a single offer, such as a deep clean before the holidays or a move-out clean for renters. Choose a goal that fits your business: either leads through an instant form or visits to a landing page. Target homeowners or renters within a sensible radius of your service area, and avoid slicing the audience too thin. Use two or three real photos or short videos of your team at work, and write a headline a neighbour would understand. Keep the form short: name, phone, postcode or suburb, and service type. Then plan to call or text new leads quickly, because cold social leads fade fast. How to judge results without being fooled

Clicks and impressions feel good but they do not pay your cleaners. Judge each channel by calls, form fills, quotes sent and jobs booked. Ask every new customer how they found you, and record the answer in one simple sheet. Give each campaign enough time to gather real data before you change it, since switching things every few days hides what is working. Compare cost per enquiry first, then cost per booked job, then the value of the customer over a year. A one-time clean and a weekly client are very different prizes, so a channel that brings recurring work can be worth more even if its first lead costs more. If you cannot answer how many leads became jobs, fix tracking before you spend more. Our guide to Google Ads versus local SEO shows how to read paid and organic numbers side by side.

Budget and timing for Calgary seasons

Cleaning demand in Calgary is not flat. Spring cleaning, pre-holiday hosting, move-out season and the end of the school year all create waves. Winter brings salt, slush and indoor living, which can lift interest in carpet and floor work. Commercial demand often follows new leases, office moves and building turnovers. Plan your spend around these waves instead of spreading it evenly. Lean in when searches rise, and pull back or shift to Meta awareness when they dip. Because costs depend on competition, season and the service you advertise, we never promise a fixed number. What we can do is set a test budget, watch real data for a few weeks and then scale what works. Targeting residential and commercial clients differently

Homeowners and facility managers behave differently, so one campaign should not chase both. For homes, Google Ads catches the person who needs a cleaner this week, while Meta can nudge busy families who have not decided yet. For offices, clinics, condo boards and property managers, search is usually stronger because they look for a supplier by name of service, such as janitorial services Calgary or office cleaning NE Calgary. Meta can still help by reaching local business owners with a clear offer for a free walkthrough. Send commercial clicks to a page that talks about insurance, schedules, supplies and reporting, not to your homepage. Our article on winning commercial cleaning contracts online goes deeper on how to shape that page and the follow-up.

Common mistakes that waste ad budget

The first mistake is sending every click to a homepage that talks about everything. The second is running ads without call tracking or form tracking, so you never learn what worked. The third is targeting the whole province when you only serve certain quadrants. The fourth is writing vague ads like quality cleaning at fair prices, which every competitor also says. The fifth is ignoring phone calls: if nobody answers during business hours, you pay for leads that go to the next company. The sixth is stopping a campaign too early, or never stopping one that clearly does not work. Fix the page first, then the tracking, then the targeting. Follow up fast and make it easy to say yes

Paid leads are expensive to earn and easy to lose. Aim to reply within minutes during business hours. Use a short, friendly script: thank them, confirm the service and area, and offer two time slots for a quote or a booking. For Meta leads, a text often works better than a call. For Google leads, a call usually wins because the person is actively searching. Add an easy way to book or request a quote online so people who prefer not to talk can still convert, which is where a cleaning booking system earns its keep. Send a reminder before the visit and a thank you after it, then ask for a review using the steps in our Google review guide. Reviews make every later ad perform better.

Using both channels together, and where Illinois fits

The strongest plan for most cleaners is layered. Google Ads captures people searching today. Meta keeps your name in front of local households who may need you next month. Local SEO and your Google profile, covered in our local SEO service, build free visibility underneath both. The same logic applies if you also serve the Fox Valley: cleaning company marketing in North Aurora, Aurora and Naperville follows the same pattern, with search for urgent jobs and social for awareness. Start with one channel, prove it, then add the second. Do not launch everything at once with a tiny budget, because you will learn nothing from any of it. Keep one person responsible for checking the numbers every week.

Run a simple weekly ad review

Pick the same time each week and spend twenty minutes on it. Check how many calls, forms and messages came in, which ads and keywords drove them, and which jobs were actually booked. Pause anything that spends money with no enquiries after a fair test. Add negative keywords when you spot wasteful searches. Refresh Meta images every few weeks so people do not tire of them. Write one line of notes on what you changed, so you can see cause and effect later. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Facebook advertising worth it for cleaning companies?

Yes, especially for residential and recurring work, when it has a clear offer and fast follow-up.

Can I run both at once?

Yes. Many cleaning companies start with Google Ads and add Meta ads once tracking is working.

What budget do I need?

It depends on your market and goals. We confirm a recommended budget after a discovery call.

Should a new cleaning company start with Google Ads or Meta ads?

If you need enquiries soon and your website is ready, start with Google Ads, because searchers already want a cleaner. If you are building a local brand or filling recurring slots, add Meta after that. It depends on your budget, service and how fast you can answer leads.

How long should I test an ad campaign before deciding?

Give it a few weeks of steady spend so you see enough calls and forms to judge. Changing settings every few days hides what is working. Watch cost per enquiry first, then how many became booked jobs.

Can I run ads in Calgary and nearby towns like Airdrie or Okotoks?

Yes. You can target specific quadrants and nearby towns. Start where your crews already travel, so you do not add long drive times, and expand once the first areas produce steady booked work.

Do ads replace local SEO for a cleaning company?

No. Ads stop when the budget stops, while local SEO and reviews keep working. Most cleaning companies do best using ads for quick leads while SEO and a strong Google profile grow in the background.
